# frame.md — Open-DTF promo (9:16)

Derived from the repo's DESIGN.md ("hoja de pruebas de imprenta"), adapted to a white video canvas.

## Color
- Canvas: `#ffffff` (sheet-white) with faint registration/grid marks in `#e8eae6`.
- Ink K (text, outlines): `#0b0b0c`. Secondary text: `#4b4b4d`.
- Process inks (full saturation for focal hits): cyan `#00aeef`, magenta `#ec008c`, yellow `#ffe600`, K `#0b0b0c`.
- Yellow is never used for text on white (contrast); it appears as fills, dots and highlights behind K text.
- Panel replica (plugin UI): bg `#2b2b2b`, field `#373737`, text `#ebe9e4`, accent `#3a9cf0`.

## Type
- Display / headlines: Bricolage Grotesque 800, tight leading 0.95 (`assets/fonts/bricolage.woff2`).
- Body / descriptions: Archivo 500 (`assets/fonts/archivo.woff2`).
- Wordmark accent: Instrument Serif italic for "Open" (`assets/fonts/instrument-serif-italic.woff2`).
- Phone scale: headlines 120–170px, descriptions 44–52px, labels 30–34px.

## Brand
- Name "Open-DTF"; mark = three growing halftone dots; "Open" serif italic + "-DTF" sans.
- No fabricated screenshots, numbers, testimonials or competitor names.

## Motion
- One continuous take: shapes morph between scenes, no hard cuts. Eases: power3/expo out for entrances, sine.inOut for ambient drift.
